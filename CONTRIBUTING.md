# Contributing to Hybrid Billing

New original contributions intended for a revision using the [source-available policy](LICENSING.md) must be explicitly offered by an authorized rightsholder under [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE), unless a different applicable grant is identified and accepted for that component. A submission against an older MIT branch does not silently change the contributor's grant. Rights already granted for earlier MIT copies and all third-party grants remain intact.

**A PolyForm contribution alone does not authorize the project to offer that contribution under separate commercial terms.** Before including externally owned material in a commercial license offering, maintainers must obtain and record the necessary separate written authorization from its rightsholder, or exclude that material from the offering. No signed contributor agreement, copyright assignment or commercial relicensing authority is created or presumed by this document. Contributors retain their copyright.

Submit only material you have the right to contribute. Identify the applicable license, prior publications, third-party sources, modifications and required notices. Preserve legal headers. Do not submit credentials, customer records, signed agreements or private operational data. Private permissions and contracts belong in the authorized contract records, with only a non-sensitive status/reference in Git where appropriate.

Follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the repository's ticket, scoped branch, review, verification and PR sequence. The planning-complete declaration does not waive per-task permissions, review or merge requirements. Maintainers review provenance and licensing scope before accepting a contribution; a merged PR by itself does not prove authority to issue a different commercial license.

For contribution or commercial-permission questions, contact [myblueton@gmail.com](mailto:myblueton@gmail.com). No contact or service purchase is required to exercise an existing MIT grant or a purpose already permitted by the applicable PolyForm license.
