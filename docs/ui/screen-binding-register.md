# Screen binding register

Tracking: Linear ticket [HB-23](https://linear.app/hybrid-billing/issue/HB-23). This register lists, for each screen whose specification is an authorized local Korean companion outside Git, the binding that a finding or contract clause requires and the local line that currently conflicts with it or lacks it.

**A register entry does not change the local specification.** The cited local text remains in force as written until it is adopted. Adoption of each entry into its specification, and publication of those specifications, belong to [HB-24](https://linear.app/hybrid-billing/issue/HB-24) under HB-15 FA-14. Every finding that depends on a local specification is therefore partially remediated, with the residual named here, not remediated.

Inputs: [HB-15 re-audit](../reviews/HB-15-full-planning/re-audit.md) FA-04, FA-05 and FA-13; [HB-15 source review](../reviews/HB-15-full-planning/source-review.md) SR-03; the `screen_followup` field of the [HB-22 source omission trace](../planning/v1.16/source-omission-trace.json) with contracts [source ERP and issuance obligations](../contracts/billing/source-erp-and-issuance-obligations.md) (EI), [source pricing and cost obligations](../contracts/billing/source-pricing-and-cost-obligations.md) (SP) and [source policy, onboarding and verification obligations](../contracts/operations/source-policy-onboarding-and-verification-obligations.md) (OP). Commands are the proposed IDs of the [planning action registry](../contracts/commands/planning-actions.md); a dash means the binding uses an existing screen action with no new command. Source locators are DOCX body-child indexes (`B`) and table rows (`.R`); they were checked against the extracted original text. Local line references are `docs/ui/screens/<ID>/spec.md:<line>` in the authorized local workspace at the time of writing. Acceptance IDs are NOT_RUN.

This is a planning register under the planning gate. It selects no customer value, threshold or timing; source examples such as D-7 stay examples, and conditional scope stays conditional.
