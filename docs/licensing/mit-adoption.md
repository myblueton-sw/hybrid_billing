# MIT adoption decision

**Historical decision:** the [2026-10-28 source-available transition](source-available-adoption.md) supersedes this as the default for current project-authored original material from that transition revision onward. Any rights already obtained for earlier MIT copies remain intact. The text below records the earlier decision; its MIT license is preserved at [LICENSES/MIT-legacy.txt](../../LICENSES/MIT-legacy.txt).

Tracking: [HB-28](https://linear.app/hybrid-billing/issue/HB-28). Accountable owner: Seung Woo Park. Machine: mac_mini. Decision date: 2026-10-23.

The owner explicitly requested that this project be open source under MIT and that commercial-use inquiries be directed to myblueton@gmail.com, before continuing the pending sample-data feature work. Root implements that decision without attaching a paid-commercial restriction to MIT.

## Decision

- Adopt the standard [MIT License](../../LICENSES/MIT-legacy.txt) for original Hybrid Billing code and project-authored documentation, subject to the explicit third-party scope in [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md).
- Permit commercial use under MIT; do not require an inquiry, license fee, registration or prior approval for the granted rights.
- Publish [myblueton@gmail.com](mailto:myblueton@gmail.com) for optional commercial-use support, consulting, integrations and collaboration. Any purchased services are separately agreed and do not narrow MIT rights.
- Preserve component-specific copyright and license notices, existing third-party grants and official FOCUS data attribution. Do not infer ownership transfer or clearance of inherited assets.

## Superseded direction and unaffected boundaries

This decision supersedes the v1.16 chapter 59 source-available, paid-commercial direction and earlier local restrictive product/evaluation drafts for original project material. Those planning inputs remain historical evidence; they do not impose conditions on the current MIT grant. The current policy and retired-draft references are reconciled in this ticket.

MIT does not expire with a commercial service subscription. Do not implement base-code entitlement or evaluation restrictions that contradict MIT. Future hosted-service access, purchased support and customer billing contracts remain separate requirements. This change does not set a service price/SLA, certify release readiness, authorize deployment or grant new merge permission.

The collective copyright notice names Hybrid Billing contributors without assigning individual or third-party rights to another party. Original third-party legal texts are preserved. Downloaded FOCUS datasets remain CC BY 4.0 and stay outside Git; independently authored synthetic code/fixtures retain the project scope described in LICENSING.md.
