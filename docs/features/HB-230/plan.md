# Scope and ownership

[HB-230](https://linear.app/hybrid-billing/issue/HB-230) starts work item W-28 (requirement HB-28; detail contracts DB-06, QA-01; HB-28-V; the snapshot parts of PT-01 and PT-02) after HB-229 (`modules/disclosure`). Base: main `0030cdc`. Root (Claude Code) owns planning, implementation and tests; a separate read-only reviewer reviews the plan and the change, because the register fixes billed amounts, document numbers and payment references. Machine: mac_mini.

Out of scope, recorded as residuals: a real PDF renderer with embedded fonts and its sandbox (this increment defines the renderer contract and the output gate and tests them with fake renderers), the `modules/exports` adapter for detail facts, currency scale profiles, tax-rate checks, yearly series resets, ERP due-date reconciliation, legal or tax certification of any document kind (the register records human tax-review evidence and never claims legal validity), business-day and holiday due-date rules, handing issued documents to `modules/receivables`, `modules/disclosure` and `modules/erpledger`, correction, cancellation and replacement of issued documents (W-29), delivery by mail or portal, persistence and screens.

# Decision

A new package `modules/issuance` follows the sibling conventions: injected clock, one mutex, workspace isolation, `ErrInvalid`, `ErrNotFound`, `ErrConflict`, `ErrRejected` (`Refusal{Reason}`), `ErrLimit`; check order form, lookup, repeat, rule, room; an identical repeat by the same actor returns the view; assistant actors are refused on every call (`ai_not_allowed`), system actors only where stated (Issue, RecordExternal, Render, Download, Preview, reads). Standard library plus `modules/protected` types.

`NewRegistry(Config{Fields, Terms, Details, Renderer, Zones}, Limits{Workspaces, Kinds, Drafts, Documents, Numbers, Attempts, Lease}, clock)`: `Numbers` bounds the values handed out per workspace (reservations, cancellations and issues together), `Attempts` the render attempts per document, and `Lease` (1 minute to 1 hour) the render lease.

- `Fields` has the `protected.Registry.Resolve` signature;
- `Terms` has `Cover(workspace, contract, at) (receivables.Coverage, error)`, a method this change adds to `modules/receivables` that applies its existing binding rules (the contract's approved binding covering the date of `at` in the contract zone; `term_missing`, `term_pending`, `term_conflict`);
- `Details` has `Detail(workspace, artifact) (DetailFacts{Digest, Rows, Total, Currency, Scale}, error)`, so the summary can only cite an attachment that the attachment register holds in the same workspace (the adapter over `modules/exports` is a residual; tests use a fake);
- `Renderer` has `Render(Snapshot) ([]byte, error)`;
- `Zones` loads IANA zones (injected for platform independence, as in `modules/receivables`).

Allowed files: `modules/issuance/**`, the additive `Coverage` type and `Cover` method with tests in `modules/receivables`, and `docs/features/HB-230/**`. Protected subjects are typed strings (`entity:<id>`, `customer:<id>`, `kind:<id>`); `modules/protected` does not enforce the convention, so registrations made under HB-23 must follow it (recorded as a residual; bare subjects fail closed as `<field>_unresolved`). Calls into `Fields`, `Terms` and `Details` happen under the issuance mutex; those registers never call back into issuance, so the lock order is one-way.

## Document kinds

`Define(workspace, Kind{ID, Class, Region, Tax, Statutory, Credit, Sequential, Zone, Languages}, actor)` proposes a kind; `ApproveKind` by another human activates it. Class is `self_issued`, `internal_settlement` or `erp_issued`. Region is two upper-case letters, Tax an identifier, Zone an IANA zone used for documents without a contract term, Languages a non-empty set of `en`, `ko`, `ar`. A statutory internal-settlement kind is `ErrInvalid`. A kind never changes after approval.

`VerifyTax(workspace, kind, evidence, actor)` records a human tax review (evidence is a reference identifier, never document content) by someone other than the kind's definer, only for approved statutory kinds. Statutory kinds issue only after it (`tax_unverified`). Snapshots carry `Statutory`, the evidence and the reviewer, so a non-statutory copy cannot be presented as a statutory document. The record is evidence that someone reviewed the treatment, not legal certification; tax amounts are not checked against rates (residual).

## Numbering responsibility and series

Documents are identified by their draft identifier; the number is an attribute, so lookups are never ambiguous between ERP, self-issued and internal numbers.

`erp_issued` kinds never receive a number from this register: `ProposeSeries`, `Reserve` and `Issue` refuse them (`erp_numbered`). `RecordExternal(workspace, draft, External{Number, IssuedAt}, actor)` (human or system) records the ERP's number (unique per kind, `external_in_use`) and the ERP's issue instant (not after now, not before the approval); the due date is computed from the contract term at that instant, as billing's expectation, and an ERP disagreement is a reconciliation item for `modules/erpledger` (residual). `RecordExternal` needs the current revision's approval and compares references and coverage exactly as `Issue` does. ERP documents are never rendered here (`not_self_issued`).

`ProposeSeries(workspace, Series{ID, Kind, Prefix, Width, First}, actor)` and `ApproveSeries` (another human) give each self-issued or internal-settlement kind exactly one series. A prefix must be unique and end in a non-digit character, so a number (prefix plus digits) determines its prefix and two series can never produce the same text. Width 1 to 12, First at least 1; a number is the prefix plus the zero-padded counter; exhausting the width is `ErrLimit`. Yearly resets are a residual.

`Reserve(workspace, draft, actor)` (human) takes the next counter value for a submitted draft; one live reservation per draft. A `Sequential` kind refuses reservations (`reservations_disabled`), so its numbers follow issue order. `Cancel(workspace, draft, reason, actor)` cancels a live reservation by a human with a reason from a closed set (`draft_abandoned`, `operator_error`, `other`); the counter value is never handed out again. An issued number cannot be cancelled here (`already_issued`). `Numbers(workspace, series)` lists every value handed out with its state (`reserved`, `issued`, `cancelled`), draft, actor, time and reason, so every value is accounted for and every gap explained. Counter changes happen under the one mutex. A number is taken only after every other issue check has passed, so a refused issue never consumes a value.

## Drafts and approval

`Submit(workspace, Draft{ID, Kind, Customer, Entity, Contract, Currency, Scale, Language, Lines, Total, Detail, Original}, actor)` records a revision by a human. Customer, Entity and Kind identifiers are at most 100 bytes, because protected subjects are typed (`entity:`, `customer:`, `kind:`). Lines are 1 to 200 `{Text, Amount, Tax}`; text is valid UTF-8 without control characters, up to 4,000 bytes. Amounts are canonical positive decimals (no sign, no leading zeros, no trailing fractional zeros, at most `Scale` fraction digits, `Scale` 0 to 6, at most 30 digits). `Total` must equal the exact sum of the lines (`total_mismatch`). The language must be one of the kind's (`language_unsupported`). Binding the scale to a currency profile is a residual.

`Detail{Artifact, Digest, Rows, Total}` links a full-detail attachment: the summary stays at most 200 lines; `Details` must return the same digest, rows, currency and scale (`detail_unverified` otherwise) and the attachment total must equal the sum of the non-tax lines (`detail_mismatch`); checked at submit and again at issue. The attachment is referenced, never embedded; its row trace is the attachment's own.

`Original` names an issued document's draft for a credit kind (`ErrInvalid` otherwise). The original's kind must be a non-credit kind of the same class with the same `Statutory` value, and the same customer, entity, currency and scale (`credit_mismatch`). The totals of all issued credits against it (including ERP-recorded ones) plus this one must not exceed its total (`exceeds_original`, checked at submit and at issue). A statutory credit kind needs its own tax review.

Each revision has a digest (label `issuance_draft_v1`). `Approve(workspace, draft, digest, actor)` by a human other than the submitter binds the approval to the digest (`digest_changed`, `self_approval`) and records the protected references and the term coverage resolved at that moment. A new revision clears approvals. When the same approver approves the same digest again, the references and coverage are re-resolved and re-recorded if they changed (this is how an approver confirms new protected values after `reference_changed`); another human gets `ErrConflict`. At issue, any reference or coverage whose change, binding or revision differs from the approved one refuses (`reference_changed`), so the approver saw the account, recipient, tax identifiers, template and term that the document will carry.

## Issue

Payable documents are non-credit self-issued and ERP-issued documents; internal and credit documents record due status `not_applicable`.

`Issue(workspace, draft, actor)` (human or system) in one critical section: kind approved and, if statutory, tax-verified; series approved; current revision approved; credit room and detail re-checked; references resolved at the issue instant (`payment_account` for `entity:` on payable documents, `document_recipient` for `customer:`, `tax_identifier` for both `entity:` and `customer:` on statutory kinds, `document_template` for `kind:`; a missing one is `<field>_unresolved`) and compared with the approval; for payable documents the coverage at the issue instant (binding, term, revision, net days, mode, zone, issue date, due date = issue date + net days, day 0 is the issue date); for others the issue date in the kind zone; then the draft's reservation is consumed or the next value taken. The snapshot (draft revision, digest, submitter, approver, issuer, number, issue instant, kind facts with tax evidence and reviewer, references with change identifiers, due facts) gets a digest (label `issuance_snapshot_v1`) and never changes. An issued draft refuses revisions (`already_issued`).

## Rendering and download

Renderer contract: it receives a deep copy of the snapshot only, embeds every asset it uses, makes no network or file-system access, reads no clock or randomness, and returns the same bytes for the same snapshot. Enforcing this with a sandbox for the real renderer is a residual; this register enforces what it can observe: determinism and the output gate.

`Render(workspace, draft, actor)` (human or system) starts an attempt with a token under the mutex, calls the renderer outside it, and records the result under the mutex again. A second attempt while one runs is refused (`render_in_progress`) unless the running one is older than the configured lease; the renderer interface has no cancellation, so an abandoned call keeps running until it returns and its result is dropped (a cancellable renderer is a residual); a completion whose token is no longer current is dropped (`attempt_superseded`). A renderer panic is recovered and recorded as `render_failed`.

The output gate tokenises the PDF object syntax instead of matching raw bytes: header `%PDF-1.` or `%PDF-2.`, trailer `%%EOF` with at most one line end after it; stream bodies are skipped using a direct `/Length` (an indirect or missing length, or a body not followed by `endstream`, is `malformed_output`); names are checked only outside stream bodies. Refused (`unsafe_output`): any `#` escape in a name; filters other than `/FlateDecode` and `/DCTDecode`; `/Encrypt`, `/ObjStm`, `/XRef`; and every name that introduces actions, links, forms, external files or media (`/A`, `/AA`, `/OpenAction`, `/Annots`, `/URI`, `/Launch`, `/JavaScript`, `/JS`, `/Names`, `/AcroForm`, `/XFA`, `/SubmitForm`, `/ResetForm`, `/ImportData`, `/GoToR`, `/GoToE`, `/GoTo3DView`, `/Named`, `/Hide`, `/SetOCGState`, `/Thread`, `/Rendition`, `/Movie`, `/Sound`, `/3D`, `/RichMedia`, `/RichMediaExecute`, `/EmbeddedFile`, `/EmbeddedFiles`, `/FS`, `/URL`, `/F` in a file specification is covered by refusing `/FS`). With no annotations, actions or name trees allowed, text strings cannot trigger requests, so strings are not inspected. Binary stream bodies containing these byte sequences pass. Files are 1 byte to 20 MiB.

So that a reader cannot find a dictionary the gate never parsed, the gate also requires exactly one classic `xref` table (one subsection starting at object 0, 20-byte entries), one `trailer` dictionary without `/Prev`, one `startxref` whose offset is the `xref` keyword, and one `%%EOF`; the table covers exactly the objects parsed; every in-use entry's offset is the start of the top-level `N G obj` with that number and generation; object numbers are unique; object 0 is free; and no stream body contains the `endstream` keyword, so a body cannot end early (`malformed_output` for all of these). Other byte sequences inside bodies are allowed: once the xref is verified, readers locate objects through it, and compressed bodies routinely contain short sequences such as `obj`.

Renderer errors are `render_failed`; the attempt is recorded and the document stays issued, so recovery is another `Render` of the same snapshot. The first accepted output fixes the file digest; a later render must reproduce it (`digest_mismatch` keeps the stored file). `Download(workspace, draft, actor)` returns the stored bytes after re-checking their digest and records the download; it never changes dates or the snapshot. `Preview(workspace, draft, actor)` returns escaped HTML without attributes (text in `bdi` elements), labelled as a viewing copy; the snapshot is the machine record. Customer-facing delivery goes through `modules/disclosure` (residual).

# Verification plan

Named cases: the tokeniser (names at boundaries, names inside strings and stream bodies, every refused name, filters, `/Encrypt`, `/ObjStm`, `/XRef`, `#` escapes, indirect and wrong `/Length`, an xref offset into a stream body, a binary body containing `obj` that must pass, duplicate object numbers, `/Prev`, trailing data, two `%%EOF`), the attempt token, lease expiry, superseded completions, renderer panics and errors, determinism, `reference_changed` and the approver refresh, `detail_unverified`, `reservations_disabled`, `erp_numbered`, non-digit prefixes and the prefix collision, `RecordExternal` approval and comparisons, and `receivables.Cover` agreeing with `Estimate`. HB-28-V row trace is satisfied only through the attachment's own trace; this register checks the attachment's digest, rows and total, and that limitation is a residual. Literal tests per gate and refusal; a property test against an independent model of numbering (random reserve, cancel, issue and concurrent goroutines: values unique, never reused, every value accounted for) and of credit room; amount and due-date tables (month end, leap day, DST zones, day-0 boundary); renderer fakes for failure, unsafe tokens, object streams, name escapes, non-determinism; `-race -count=10`, a 386 build, gofmt, vet, the whole repository with `-race`, 100% statement coverage, mutation testing.

# Change review resolution

Change review round 1: FAIL (3 major, 11 minor). No defects were found in amounts, numbering, approval binding, tenant isolation or actor rules. Resolved:

- M1: after `startxref` only one line end and `%%EOF` may follow; any comment containing `EOF` or `startxref` is refused, so a backward search cannot find a second `startxref`.
- M2: `XRefStm` and `Prev` are refused everywhere (document outlines, which use `/Prev`, are therefore not supported by this gate); every `N G R` reference must name an in-use object of the verified table with the same generation; the trailer must carry `/Size` equal to the table length and `/Root` pointing to a `/Type /Catalog` object whose `/Pages` points to a `/Type /Pages` object.
- M3: `F`, `FFilter`, `FDecodeParms`, `AF`, `Filespec`, `UF`, `EF`, `Ref`, `OPI` and `Alternates` are refused; `/Filter` values are an allow-list (`FlateDecode`, `DCTDecode`, or an array of them) wherever the key appears.
- Minors: `Define` sorts before the repeat comparison; the attempt limit is checked before a lease expiry supersedes the running attempt; the renderer output is copied before inspection and hashing; the zone is resolved once before a number is taken (`zone_unavailable`) and passed to the due computation; `Coverage.Date` and `Due` must equal the recomputed values (`coverage_mismatch`); numbers keep the reserver and reservation time (`Taker`, `Taken`); the snapshot records and digests the issuer kind and repeats compare it; downloads are recorded with actor, kind and time under a `Downloads` limit; `/Length` must appear exactly once at the stream dictionary's top level with a numeric value.
- Deviation recorded: read calls (`KindOf`, `DraftOf`, `Numbers`, `DocumentOf`) take no actor, matching the sibling registers; actor checks on reads belong to the access layer (residual).
- Residual: lenient readers that enter repair mode on files this gate accepts could still index objects inside stream bodies; the gate's xref, trailer and catalog checks make that unnecessary for conforming readers, and sandboxing the viewer is outside this register.

Change review round 2: FAIL (1 major, 3 minor), all round-1 findings resolved. Resolved: document views copy the download records (N1, with a test that changes a returned record); `Root`, `Pages`, `Size` and `Type` are read only from an object's or the trailer's own top-level dictionary and must appear exactly once; a second `stream` in one object is refused. Provisional, for the owner: `Limits.Downloads` caps the recorded downloads of one document and then refuses further downloads; moving download records to an append-only audit store is a residual.

Change review round 3: PASS. Its one minor follow-up is applied: `key()` stops at the end of the first top-level dictionary, with a test for a second top-level dictionary in the catalog object.
