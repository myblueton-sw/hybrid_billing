# Commercial and evaluation inquiries

Original Hybrid Billing material is available under [MIT](../../LICENSE), including commercial use and evaluation. No separate evaluation agreement, fee or permission is required to exercise that grant.

Contact [myblueton@gmail.com](mailto:myblueton@gmail.com) for optional support, consulting, integration assistance, collaboration or a separately agreed service engagement. Publishing this contact creates no price, SLA or contractual commitment. Any purchased service defines its own scope, duration, fees, confidentiality and responsibilities; it does not restrict MIT rights in the software.

Earlier plans for mandatory paid commercial licenses, commercial evaluation restrictions and base-software license-expiry gates are superseded by the [MIT decision](mit-adoption.md). They must not be implemented as conditions on the MIT-licensed code. Future hosted-service subscription access or support entitlement is a separate product contract, not a restriction on copying, modifying or commercially using this software.

Customer records, credentials and signed agreements must stay outside Git. Existing third-party licenses and data attribution remain applicable. See [current policy](product-policy.md), [licensing scope](../../LICENSING.md) and [contribution terms](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
