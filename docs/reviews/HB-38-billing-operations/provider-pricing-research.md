# Scope and conclusion

[HB-38](https://linear.app/hybrid-billing/issue/HB-38/redesign-monthly-billing-operations-for-1000-customer-msp-usability), researched 2026-10-28. Root integrates primary-document research; separate researchers covered AWS/Azure, GCP and Alibaba/IBM. OCI is included in the core AWS/Azure/GCP/OCI/Alibaba comparison. IBM is supplementary; VMware requires a separate license and infrastructure cost basis. No private contract, authenticated provider export or customer account was inspected.

**A provider's exported cost is often already discounted. An enterprise agreement is not a percentage to apply again to every exported row.** Exported observations, contractual expectations, supplier settlement and MSP customer selling rules need separate evidence and accounting meaning. An agreement may specify fixed prices, eligible SKUs, thresholds, price protection, minimum spend, commitment terms or rebates rather than one universal discount.

The existing [source-cost obligations](../../contracts/billing/source-pricing-and-cost-obligations.md) and [pricing sequence](../../contracts/billing/inputs-pricing-and-erp.md) remain authoritative. This report supplies adapter and screen design evidence, not certified integrations or a new financial engine.

| Provider | What the source can establish | Additional evidence | Main double-counting risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AWS | Usage, conditional net costs, discount representations, RI/SP allocations | Actual CUR/export configuration, agreement, eligibility and invoice/credits | Applying EDP again to net cost; counting both discount rows and a discount map |
| Azure | Negotiated/effective usage prices; separate partner reconciliation | EA/MCA/partner schema, invoice, credit and MACC evidence | Mixing Actual and Amortized; interpreting MACC as another discount |
| GCP | Negotiated usage cost, signed credits, account pricing and reseller customer cost | Export dates, consumption model, repricing configuration, invoice | Reapplying negotiated discount; applying reseller margin to customer charges |
| OCI | Discounted usage cost, rate-card metadata, commitment usage/balances | Subscription rate card, monthly statement, service/usage invoice | Adding overage twice or treating prepaid consumption as new cash payable |
| Alibaba | Original, discounted and payable amounts; coupons and adjustments | Bill granularity, partner settlement, invoice | Counting the same funded coupon in both customer and supplier settlement |

# AWS: enterprise discounts, CUR and pro forma costs

[CUR line items](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cur/latest/userguide/Lineitem-columns.html) expose `lineItem/UnblendedCost` and conditional `lineItem/NetUnblendedCost` / `lineItem/NetUnblendedRate`. Net values reflect discounts; a missing conditional column is not a zero price or proof of no agreement. Preserve line type, seller, currency and account identity. [Discount columns](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cur/latest/userguide/discount-details.html) include `discount/BundledDiscount` and `discount/TotalDiscount` where applicable.

[CUR 2.0 discount fields](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cur/latest/userguide/table-dictionary-cur2-discount.html) include a named `discount` map, `discount_bundled_discount` and `discount_total_discount`. [Export configuration](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cur/latest/userguide/table-dictionary-cur2.html) changes representation: `INCLUDE_MANUAL_DISCOUNT_COMPATIBILITY` can move discounts to separate rows. Do not aggregate both representations. These documents do not establish `EdpDiscount` or `PrivateRateDiscount` as universal keys. Do not mechanically translate every legacy slash-named field into an assumed CUR 2.0 column.

Record the actual [GetTable schema](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-cost-management/latest/APIReference/API_DataExports_GetTable.html) and [GetExport selection/configuration](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-cost-management/latest/APIReference/API_DataExports_GetExport.html), account scope, period, output format and correction revision. Public documentation alone does not establish which selected fields an installation receives. Even documentation naming differences require actual schema validation.

An EDP agreement's eligible spend, rate schedule, exclusions, term and commitment progress require its evidence. A list-to-net difference does not identify its legal cause. Enterprise spend commitments must be distinguished from resource commitments: [reservation fields](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cur/latest/userguide/reservation-columns.html) and [Savings Plans fields](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cur/latest/userguide/savingsplans-columns.html) support fee/effective-use analysis, but do not authorize charging unused commitment to arbitrary customers. Purchase/fee and allocated effective usage are alternative accounting views, not amounts to add indiscriminately.

[Billing Conductor pro forma](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/billingconductor/latest/userguide/understanding-proforma.html) represents a separate modeled billing view, with differences from AWS actual charges and benefit sharing. It is not proof of the supplier payable. Import it with a distinct basis and provenance; do not reconcile pro forma directly as though it were the supplier invoice.

# Azure: agreement type changes the dataset

In [EA cost details](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/dataset-schema/cost-usage-details-ea), `UnitPrice` already represents negotiated pricing; `EffectivePrice` is the effective period price. `CostInBillingCurrency` excludes credits and taxes. Reservation identity and quantity remain necessary. In [MCA details](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/dataset-schema/cost-usage-details-mca), inspect version-specific `invoiceId`, `previousInvoiceId`, `unitPrice`, `effectivePrice`, `PayGPrice` (for example, in `2023-12-01-preview`), `costInBillingCurrency`, pricing currency/FX, charge type and benefit identity. An Azure-credit eligibility indicator does not prove credit application. Pin the actual schema version and casing rather than mixing examples across versions.

[Partner Center reconciliation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/billing/modern-invoice-reconciliation-file) is a separate CSP purchasing source. Relevant fields include `UnitPrice`, `EffectiveUnitPrice`, `BillableQuantity`, `PriceAdjustmentDescription`, `Subtotal`, `TaxTotal`, `Total`, `ChargeType`, `CreditReasonCode`, `PricingCurrency` and `PCToBCExchangeRate`. Preserve published adjustments and totals; a manual post-billing credit is not necessarily reproducible with the ordinary quantity-times-price equation.

[MACC tracking](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/benefits/macc/track-consumption-commitment) concerns eligible commitment consumption, not an additional percentage discount. Credit-funded consumption and prepayment purchases have specific treatment; do not count a qualifying purchase and subsequent funded usage twice. Actual and Amortized data serve different financial views and must never be summed into one bill.

# GCP: negotiated prices, credits and reseller pricing coexist

[Detailed usage export](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/export-data-bigquery-tables/detailed-usage) distinguishes `cost`, `cost_at_list`, `cost_at_effective_price_default` and `cost_at_list_consumption_model`; the consumption model matters. Price fields include `price.list_price`, `price.effective_price_default`, `price.list_price_consumption_model` and `price.effective_price`. Do not invent a bare `cost_at_effective_price` field. Older rows may lack newer fields: current model-comparison fields became available in July 2025, and detailed price fields have a different history. Absence is not zero or permission to reconstruct historical contract prices from today's list.

[Standard export](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/export-data-bigquery-tables/standard-usage) includes signed `credits.amount`, identity/type and `adjustment_info`. Credit types distinguish contractual discounts, promotions, reseller margin, resource CUDs, legacy spend CUDs and `FEE_UTILIZATION_OFFSET`. A credit ID can identify a category rather than a unique financial event; it is not a sufficient global deduplication key. Net cost uses cost plus signed credits without losing tax, rounding or adjustment rows. Preserve reversal/replacement relationships.

[Pricing export](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/export-data-bigquery-tables/pricing-data) provides account-specific tiered rates, list rates, consumption-model prices and `price_info` reasons/discount metadata. This can evidence fixed prices, price protection and current- or historical-list discounts. `pricing_as_of_time` is an observation timestamp, not automatically the agreement's effective start. A missing account price is not a zero contract price. [Export limitations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/export-data-bigquery) mean pricing history before export enablement cannot simply be assumed available.

[New spend-based CUDs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/cuds-multiprice) can put the discounted price directly on covered usage and use a fee offset, whereas legacy representations use other credit types. Synthetic example: usage 80 + commitment fee 100 − utilization offset 80 = 100, including 20 unused commitment. Account/product migration matters; not every CUD uses the new model. The Preview [CUD metadata export](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/export-data-bigquery-tables/cud-export) supplies commitment identity, scope, terms and fee SKU, but is not a complete enterprise agreement ledger.

[Reseller export](https://docs.cloud.google.com/channel/docs/rebilling/export-data-to-bigquery) explicitly separates supplier `cost`/`credits.amount`, `customer_cost`/`credits.customer_amount`, and channel-partner equivalents. Preserve payer/customer/entitlement identity and repricing configuration. Reseller margin is not customer credit. A missing repricing configuration name can occur for Marketplace private offers and does not prove absence of customer pricing. [Repricing configuration](https://docs.cloud.google.com/channel/docs/rebilling/manage-repricing-configurations) supports bases, SKU groups, percentage changes and dates; propagation and mid-month adjustment limits affect reconciliation.

[Cost-table guidance](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/cost-table) includes contract billing adjustments and promotion-specific behavior. [Invoice discrepancy guidance](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/resolve-issues) distinguishes usage timing, invoice month and corrections. Exported adjustments do not establish where or when a credit memo was applied; billing-document evidence is required. Credit memo issuance/application and a cash refund remain separate facts.

# OCI: rate cards, Universal Credits and invoices

[Native and FOCUS cost reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/costusagereportsoverview.htm) expose `cost/subscriptionId`, `cost/productSku`, `cost/unitPrice`, `cost/myCost`, overage fields and correction references. `myCost` already includes overage: never add `myCostOverage` again. FOCUS `BilledCost` and `EffectiveCost` map to discounted `myCost`; tax and invoice timing can differ. Null commitment-discount fields do not establish absence of a Universal Credits agreement. Current-list fields are not historical contract evidence. Preserve correction chains. The table and prose differ in correction-field spelling, so validate actual headers. Dedicated GPU pool exceptions also make a universal quantity-times-price check unsafe. Reports can arrive late and do not cover non-metered tenancies.

[RateCardSummary](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/tools/python/latest/api/onesubscription/models/oci.onesubscription.models.RateCardSummary.html) documents `net_unit_price`, `discretionary_discount_percentage`, `overage_price`, `is_tier`, `rate_card_tiers`, `subscribed_service_id`, `time_start`, `time_end`, currency and product. Thus OCI does expose some contract-rate metadata; it is inaccurate to say all negotiated details exist only in PDFs. Actual API entitlement and account availability remain unverified. SDK model documentation alone is not proof of successful collection.

[ComputedUsageSummary](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/tools/python/latest/api/onesubscription/models/oci.onesubscription.models.ComputedUsageSummary.html) provides usage identity, original usage, commitment/rate-card links, net price, cost/rounded cost, invoiced status and arrival/metering times. Types distinguish usage, overage, promotion, service credit, funded allocation, commitment expiration and monthly minimum. Treat these as typed events, not one generic discount amount. Verify API family and exact field names against the selected SDK and response.

[Universal Credits](https://www.oracle.com/cloud/universal-credits/) combine a prepaid commitment and eligible consumption at applicable rates. Contract-specific eligibility, expiry, overage and partner terms still require agreement evidence. Prepaid consumption is not automatically another cash payment, and unused commitment is not automatically refundable or customer-chargeable.

[Monthly usage statements](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/usagestatements.htm) are parent-tenancy XLSX documents. They include commitment identifiers, term, opening/closing balances and SKU usage/overage amounts. Statements exclude tax, can cover a different period from invoices and can be revised after delayed usage. A missing statement for zero usage must not be interpreted as failed billing without checking applicability.

[OCI invoices](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/invoices.htm) distinguish service commitment billing from usage/overage billing and show credit/payment application. Reconcile consumption, commitment movement and invoice settlement separately. [Organization reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/General/organization/organization_cost_reporting.htm) adds parent/child scope and subscription reassignment history. Use historical effective mappings, not today's tenancy assignment for every prior month.

# Alibaba: discounts, coupons and settlement roles

[DescribeInstanceBill](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/user-center/developer-reference/api-bssopenapi-2017-12-14-describeinstancebill) exposes `PretaxGrossAmount`, `InvoiceDiscount`, `AfterDiscountAmount`, `DeductedByCoupons`, `PretaxAmount`, item types and billing/owner identities. Item-level list price, usage and resource-package details depend on `IsBillingItem=true`. Do not infer a universal coupon equation from ambiguous field prose. Current-month data is provisional; delayed/unsettled usage prevents treating it as finalized reconciliation evidence. Validate the documented closing schedule and actual final export.

[QueryBillOverview](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/user-center/developer-reference/api-bssopenapi-2017-12-14-querybilloverview) provides tax, local-currency, gross, coupon and outstanding fields. Outstanding/credit-limit settlement is not promotional credit. [Channel roles](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/partner/channel?_p_lc=1) distinguish reseller purchasing from agency commission: the commercial role determines which amount is supplier cost versus customer payable.

[Partner billing](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/channel-platform/latest/briefinstruction) and [coupon distribution](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/channel-platform/latest/coupondistributionmanagement) show role-specific discounts and funded coupons. An Alibaba-funded PoC benefit can appear as a customer-to-partner coupon deduction and a partner-to-Alibaba discount deduction. They are two settlement views of one benefit, not two deductions from the same customer charge. Preserve funder, beneficiary, settlement layer, eligibility, validity and balance.

# Supplementary evidence and VMware boundary

[IBM usage reports](https://cloud.ibm.com/docs/account?topic=account-understand-reports) distinguish original `rated_cost`, volume discounts, resulting `cost`, offer credits/validity and subscription agreement/term/balance/overage. [Enterprise billing](https://cloud.ibm.com/docs/enterprise-management?topic=enterprise-management-enterprise) can consolidate child accounts and consume subscription credits at discounted rates. [Usage visibility](https://cloud.ibm.com/docs/account?topic=account-viewingusage) and [billing FAQs](https://cloud.ibm.com/docs/account?locale=en&topic=account-billusagefaqs) explain why usage exports and account-level credits need invoice reconciliation. These findings do not add IBM to committed core product coverage.

[Broadcom legal terms](https://www.broadcom.com/company/legal/licensing) and [VCSP program information](https://knowledge.broadcom.com/external/article/374738/broadcom-vscp-vmware-cloud-services-prov.html) require the actual commercial agreement and applicable product terms. This research did not establish a universal VMware billing export. Design inference: vCenter measurements are usage evidence, not license purchase cost; hardware, support, power and shared infrastructure require explicit cost inputs and allocation policy.

# Cross-provider normalization and release evidence

[FOCUS ContractedCost](https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-3/columns/cost-and-usage/contracted-cost/) and [cost comparisons](https://focus.finops.org/docs/specification/v1-4/features/cost-comparison/) distinguish negotiated/list/billed/effective views. Pin each provider's delivered schema and the product's chosen standard version; a newer specification does not establish provider support. Preserve native extensions and unavailable-field reasons. Never overwrite normalized supplier cost with MSP selling price.

Before certifying an adapter, require a permitted representative export, actual schema/configuration, applicable rate card, invoice/credit evidence and a reconciliation across one closed period. Include a late correction, expired/partial credit, account reassignment, commitment purchase/usage and excluded SKU where applicable. Public research does not resolve an installation's private prices, resale rights, unused-commitment bearer, tax treatment or document access permissions.
