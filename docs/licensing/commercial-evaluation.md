# Commercial and evaluation inquiries

For material covered by the current [PolyForm policy](product-policy.md), use within the license's permitted purposes needs no separate commercial agreement. Do not assume that every evaluation is noncommercial: anticipated commercial application and the actual purpose matter under the selected license. The license's explicit personal-use and organizational permissions remain unchanged.

Commercial use outside the applicable grant requires separate permission. Contact [myblueton@gmail.com](mailto:myblueton@gmail.com) to discuss the relevant versions, use, authorized rightsholders and contractual scope. An inquiry or download does not create a commercial license, evaluation period, fee, service level or signed agreement. Supporting services can be agreed separately from software permissions.

Any rights already obtained for an earlier MIT copy, including commercial rights, remain unaffected under its [historical MIT grant](../../LICENSES/MIT-legacy.txt). This transition adds no contact, payment or evaluation condition to those earlier rights and does not offer the current revision under MIT. Third-party and external-contributor rights also remain separate. The project cannot promise commercial rights it has not obtained.

No activation, remote locking, automatic external use reporting or payment execution is authorized by this document. Customer records, credentials and signed contracts stay outside Git. See the [scope](../../LICENSING.md), [contribution terms](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [current decision](source-available-adoption.md).
